## 2. Logic areas

### LA-1 Restated usage reports (silver usage_report)

Dimensions: the number of reports for one data provider, subscriber, product and month (one, or two or more); and, with two or more, whether the latest declared_at is unique or shared by two reports. Brute force 4. Collapse: with one report two equal times cannot occur, so 3 paths. Design D-2: one report per month counts.

### LA-2 Keys conformed to the platform (silver license_line)

Dimensions: the Account's Tenant__c and Subscriber_Id__c name a platform subscriber (yes or no); the Product2's Tenant__c and ProductCode name a platform product (yes or no). Brute force 4; no collapse, since each pair fails on its own. Design D-19.

### LA-3 The license chain (silver license_line)

Dimensions: for a line item, its Opportunity, Contract, Account and Product2 each present (yes or no). Brute force 16. Collapse: any missing piece drops the line item, whichever it is, so the paths are all present and each one missing alone: 5. Design D-7.

### LA-4 Change time (silver invoice_item)

Dimensions: the invoice's updated_at against its item's: later, earlier or equal. Brute force 3; no collapse. Design D-17.

### LA-5 and LA-6 Product lookup (silver invoice_item, silver entitlement)

Dimension: the row's product_id names a platform product (yes or no). Brute force 2 each; no collapse. Design D-11, D-19.

### LA-7 Deleted at the source; LA-8 change hash (every silver table)

LA-7: the staging row is marked deleted (yes or no), 2 paths, one test on subscriber standing for the shared filter. LA-8: a non-key column changes (yes or no), 2 paths, one test on product standing for the shared expression. Design D-19, D-20.

### LA-9 The five checks; LA-10 coverage and filters (gold, increment 2)

LA-9: the source profile's round one, 80 combinations collapsed to the 14 paths of profile result P-6 (design D-12). LA-10: seven filters, each one document's (license covers the month, Contract Activated, Opportunity won, entitlement covers the month, invoice in the month, invoice not void, latest usage report): brute force 128; each failing filter only removes its own document, so the paths are all passing and each one failing alone: 8 (D-7, D-16). Their tests are named in the path table below (increment 2 guide, knowledge 111 item 816).
