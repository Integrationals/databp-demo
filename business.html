DataBP demo / The business

Market data: the business behind the demo

An exchange sells market data through a vendor to a subscriber. Four documents describe each subscriber; the app asks whether they agree, and what changed since the last close.

#ConceptWhat it is
1Market dataThe prices and trades financial markets produce, sold to investment firms
2LicenseThe right an exchange grants in its market data agreement
3VendorControls which subscribers receive the exchange's data
4Subscriber and entitlementThe firm receiving data; the vendor's permission to receive it
5Counting the useUnit of count; the monthly usage report; the declaration of use

Sources: TRG Screen, on revenue management for exchanges; the MEMX Market Data Agreement, section 4; an NYSE filing of 2010-05-12 on subscriber entitlement; Nasdaq European Data Policies and CME Group, on unit of count; Euronext Reporting Guidelines v1.6, section 2.3; the ICE Market Data Vendor Guide, section 2.4.1.
